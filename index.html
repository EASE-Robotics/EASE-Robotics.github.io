About the Competition

EASE Robotics is a season-long build-and-compete program for middle and high school teams. Every registered team receives a starter kit and works at their own pace through guided tutorials.

Teams submit a video of their robot completing the season’s challenge, get scored by our judges, and climb the public leaderboard as the season progresses.
